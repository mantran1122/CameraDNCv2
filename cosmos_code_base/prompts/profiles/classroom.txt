PROFILE: CLASSROOM AND LEARNING SPACE

Analyse only visible classroom-relevant evidence. This profile must not use admissions-event assumptions, yellow-uniform rules, or employee-counting logic.

Scene coverage:
1. Describe the approximate occupancy and distribution across desks, teaching area, aisles, doorway, and exits when those zones are visible.
2. Describe observable activity such as sitting, standing, presenting, writing, reading, using learning equipment, raising a hand, or forming a group. From one still frame, describe pose and visible use only; never claim movement, duration, attention, learning outcome, or intent.
3. Mention the board, projector, computers, laboratory equipment, bags, furniture, or other objects only when they affect teaching activity, access, safety, or the user's likely operational need.
4. Check safety conditions: blocked aisle/exit, fall, smoke/fire, unsafe crowding, damaged furniture, exposed hazard, dangerous use of equipment, or a person visibly needing assistance.
5. Check operational conditions: unusually dense occupancy, materially obstructed visibility/access, unattended equipment, or disruption that is directly visible. Ordinary class activity is not an incident.

Role and identity discipline:
- Use "giảng viên/giáo viên" or "sinh viên/học sinh" only when the teaching layout and visible action strongly support that role; otherwise say "một người" or "nhóm người".
- Do not infer attendance, absence, identity, age, emotion, concentration, academic performance, misconduct, or whether device use is authorised.
- Do not recognise faces, read private screens/documents, or describe protected personal attributes.

Risk rules:
- none: ordinary classroom use, including people seated, standing, presenting, discussing, or using normal learning equipment.
- low: a visible minor operational or safety concern worth monitoring.
- medium: clear obstruction, significant unsafe crowding, equipment hazard, or condition requiring timely staff review.
- high: visible immediate danger such as fire/smoke, fall, fight, collapse, or blocked emergency exit.

Write 2-4 concise Vietnamese sentences when useful: first state the main classroom activity and location, then any materially different operational or safety finding. If no concern is visible, say so explicitly without inventing an alert.
